    Proportions are locked. Change either value and the other updates.

    Cut line shape

    Smoother outlines bridge the small gaps between star points and sparkles. The tool paints those bridged areas white, so each sticker keeps a clean white edge.

    mm

    How far the cut sits outside the design. Use 1.5 to 3 mm for a white-border sticker. A negative value cuts inside the edge: −0.5 mm cuts just inside a printed white border.

    mm

    Higher values give fewer nodes and softer curves. Every node is smooth, so the blade never has to stop and turn. At 4 the line stays within 0.21 mm of the edge; at 8, within 0.37 mm.

    mm

    Bridges gaps between separate letters or parts so you get one outline. Set 0 to turn it off.

    mm

    Minimum radius for inward corners and notches. Printers usually ask for 1 to 3 mm. The gap already rounds outer corners. Parts narrower than 2 mm are shown in orange.

    Border & bleed
    mm

    The print extends this far beyond the cut so small cutting shifts don't leave a gap. 2 to 3 mm is typical. Many printers ask for 3 mm (⅛").

    mm

    Shown as a dashed green line. Keep text and logos inside it. Most printers ask for 3 mm (⅛").

    Cut lines
    pt

    The cut line is a stroke-only, overprinting spot colour on its own layer. Set the width to 0 for a hairline.

    mm
    mm

    The main line becomes the kiss cut, through the sticker only. This second line, shown in blue, cuts through the backing as well. It gets its own spot colour and layer. Check the name with your printer or RIP.

    Optional extras

    Artwork style

    Higher detail keeps small shapes and sharper edges, with more nodes. Lower is cleaner and lighter.

    Vector shapes turn flat-colour artwork (logos, text, solid-colour cartoons) into editable filled curves in the PDF, SVG and EPS. Photos and 3D-shaded art come out blotchy, so keep those as an image.

    White ink layer

    For clear, transparent or metallic vinyl. Roland VersaWorks prints white ink wherever the spot colour RDG_WHITE is used. The layer is placed above the artwork and set to overprint, shown in violet in the preview.

    Background removal

    Raise this to ignore soft drop shadows and glows.

    mm²

    On sticker sheets, 25 mm² (5 × 5 mm) drops stray logo or text bits without losing real stickers.
