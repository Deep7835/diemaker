Privacy policy

Effective 24 October 2026Last updated 24 October 2026

Die Line Maker is a free tool that makes die cut lines from sticker and label images. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the website. In short: your images never leave your device, and we don't collect personal data.

Your images and designs

When you upload an image, it is opened and processed by your own web browser, on your own computer or phone. The image, the cut line and the files you download (PDF, SVG, EPS, DXF, PNG, ZIP) are created on your device. They are not sent to us or to anyone else, and we can't see them.

When you close or reload the page, the image is gone. We don't keep a copy, because we never had one.

What we collect

There are no accounts, sign-ups, forms or payments on this site, so we don't ask for your name, email address, phone number or any other personal details.

The site doesn't use analytics, advertising or tracking scripts.

Cookies and local storage

The site doesn't set cookies. If you use the light or dark mode switch, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage under the name dlm-theme, so the site looks the same next time. It stays on your device and is never sent to us. You can clear it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Because we don't use tracking or advertising cookies, the site doesn't show a cookie banner.

Hosting

Like every website, our hosting provider receives basic technical information when your browser asks for a page: your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time. The host uses this to deliver the site and protect it from abuse, and keeps it only for a short time under its own privacy policy. We don't use it to identify you.

Styles and scripts are served from our own website. The General Sans font comes from Fontshare, run by Indian Type Foundry, so your browser downloads it from their servers. Like any web server, they receive your IP address and browser type when it does. We don't send them anything else.

Links to other websites

Our guides link to other websites, such as software makers' manuals. Those sites have their own privacy policies, which apply once you leave Die Line Maker.

Your rights

We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Since we don't collect personal data, there is usually nothing for us to show, correct or delete. If you think we hold information about you, or you have a question or complaint about privacy, email [add your email to site.config.json] and we'll reply within 30 days.

Children

The site isn't aimed at children, and we don't knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the site works, for example by adding a feature that needs personal data, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.

Contact

For anything about this policy, email [add your email to site.config.json].
